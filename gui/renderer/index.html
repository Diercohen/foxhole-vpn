Mozilla's VPN service is blocked here

It seems your government or internet provider blocks Firefox's VPN service (vpn.mozilla.org) in your country.

Set up a relay to get around it: a free script on your own Google account that reaches it for Foxhole. It takes a few minutes, and Foxhole shows you each click.

Relay: your own way through

A free script on your own Google account fetches the web for you. Your internet provider sees only a visit to Google, so Relay gets through where Firefox VPN is blocked (e.g. Iran), and lets Foxhole reach Firefox VPN there too.

It carries web pages only, and sites can still see your IP. Setting it up takes a few minutes, and Foxhole shows you each click.
